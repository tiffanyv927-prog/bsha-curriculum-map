SME Instructions: This view shows how the BSHA is designed to feed into the MBA-HA. Students exit the BSHA at Competent (Apply/Analyze/Evaluate) and enter the MBA-HA at Proficient (Analyze/Evaluate/Create). In the MBA-HA, students choose one of three emphasis tracks: Healthcare Finance & Accounting (aligned to HFMA CHFP credential), Long-Term Care Administration (aligned to NAB LNHA licensure), or Supply Chain Management (aligned to ASCM CSCP credential). Each track includes track-differentiated versions of AMT2 and AFT2, one new track-specific course (NEW-F, NEW-L, or NEW-S), and a track-specific C219 capstone. Your job: tell us if this bridge is the right size, in the right direction, and whether these three MBA-HA tracks serve your industry's needs — or whether a different specialization would be more valuable.
